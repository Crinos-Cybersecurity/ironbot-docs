# Security Policy

IronBOT is a security product, so we hold our own security to the same standard we ask of our users. If you believe you've found a vulnerability in IronBOT, our infrastructure, or this project, we want to hear about it.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Report privately through either channel:

- Email: **contato@ironbot.io**
- Contact form: [ironbot.io/#contact](https://ironbot.io/#contact) (please mention "Security" in the subject)

Include as much of the following as you can:

- A clear description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce (proof of concept, request/response, screenshots)
- The affected component (web app, API, infrastructure, documentation)
- Any suggested remediation

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement** within 2 business days.
- **Assessment and status update** within 7 business days.
- We will keep you informed as we work on a fix, and we'll credit you in the resolution note if you'd like.

## Scope

In scope:

- `app.ironbot.io` and related IronBOT services
- `ironbot.io` and this repository's content

Out of scope:

- Denial-of-service testing, spam, or social engineering against our team
- Automated scanner output without a demonstrated, reproducible impact
- Third-party services we don't control

## Our commitment

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who:

- Act in good faith
- Test only against accounts and targets they own or are authorized to test
- Report findings privately and give us reasonable time to fix them before disclosure

Thank you for helping keep IronBOT — and everyone who depends on it — safe.
